Help

How can we help?

Quick answers for the things you may need.

What is Cookie?

Cookie is a simple AI workspace designed to help you write, learn, plan, and explore ideas without getting in the way.

How do I start a new chat?

Tap New chat in the sidebar, or use the new-chat button in the top bar on mobile.

Can I change the appearance?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Dark, Light, or System mode.

Is my data stored?

This frontend demo keeps only the small amount of state needed for the interface in your browser. A production backend can later add secure account storage.
