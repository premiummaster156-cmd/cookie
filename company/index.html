02 / Principles

Confidence needs evidence.

We want AI that answers clearly when evidence supports an answer—and makes uncertainty visible when it does not.

Independent model development

Kuki's core inference uses its own trained weights rather than secretly substituting another company's hosted chat model.

Truth over appearance

We separate ambitions from measured results. The goal is reliable answers, not confident-sounding guesses.

Safety at the edge

Cloudflare protections such as DDoS mitigation, bot checks, CAPTCHA, and rate limits support the service around the model.

Measured releases

New checkpoints should be compared on math, reasoning, language, coding, factuality, and multi-turn dialogue before promotion.
